# PX POS Privacy Policy

_Draft for Mandy's approval. Once approved it's published at https://px-pos.vercel.app/privacy and linked from Google Play._

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

PX POS is restaurant billing software made by PostX ("we"). It has two parts: the PX POS app, which runs on an outlet's tills, and PX POS Admin, the website the outlet is managed from. This policy explains what PX POS stores and why.

## Who is responsible

Each restaurant or café using PX POS (the "outlet") decides what it records about its customers and staff. PostX runs the software and stores that information for the outlet. Questions about an outlet's own records can go to the outlet or to us.

## What PX POS stores

- **Orders and bills:** items, prices, taxes, totals, how each bill was paid, bill and token numbers, and dates and times.
- **Customer details, only when the outlet enters them:** a name on an order, and the phone number and address for deliveries. A business customer's GSTIN when the bill needs it.
- **Staff:** name, role, and a scrambled (hashed) form of each person's PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.
- **The outlet's setup:** menu, prices, GST details, address, FSSAI number, receipt settings and logo.
- **The till:** an app ID created when the till is paired, the device's name and model, the app version, and when the till was last online.

PX POS doesn't use the device's location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It shows no ads and doesn't track people across apps or websites.

## Why

Only to run the outlet's billing: taking orders, printing bills and kitchen tickets, keeping sales safe while offline, end-of-day reports, and showing the outlet its sales in PX POS Admin.

## Where it's kept

The data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in Mumbai, India (asia-south1), and is sent over encrypted connections. Each till also keeps a copy of recent sales on the device so it can work without the internet.

Only the outlet's owner, its staff on the outlet's tills, the PostX admins assigned to that outlet, and PostX itself can read an outlet's data.

## Sharing

We don't sell or rent anyone's data, and we don't share it with advertisers. Google stores the data for us as our service provider. We only disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long it's kept

Bills are kept as long as the outlet uses PX POS, because GST law requires businesses to keep tax records for several years. Customer details can be removed on request (see below).

## Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your name, phone number or address removed, email **adxbypostx@gmail.com** or ask the outlet. We'll reply within 30 days. Tax details on bills that were already issued may have to be kept to meet GST law.

## Children

PX POS is a business tool for adults. It isn't meant for children and doesn't knowingly collect children's data.
